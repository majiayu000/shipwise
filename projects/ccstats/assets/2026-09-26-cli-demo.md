Captured on 2026-09-26 from the `v0.9.0` macOS arm64 release binary. The
release archive passed its published SHA-256 checksum, and `ccstats --version`
printed `ccstats 0.9.0`.

This is actual CLI output over a **synthetic Codex fixture**, not a screenshot
of anyone's private usage. The numbers below are sample data and must not be
presented as user metrics or a benchmark.

The isolated `CODEX_HOME` contained one `token_count` record with 100 input,
20 cached input, 30 output, and 10 reasoning output tokens. The input and
